# NSRUDP Nauru Web Map — Data Notice & Terms of Use

_Last updated: August 2026_

## What this map shows

Water supply infrastructure and base mapping for Nauru, created under the
NSRUDP. The water reticulation design was prepared by BICL under the
NSRUDP. Layers include the designed reticulation network, fittings and
valves, the old (abandoned) pipe system, cadastral parcels, district
boundaries, 1 m contours, fuel lines, railway, and RO intake works.

**All water reticulation content on this map is design information only.
It is not an "as constructed" record, and no part of it should be read as
showing what was actually built.**

## Important limitations — read before relying on this data

- **Design data, not "as constructed".** The reticulation network,
  fittings, and related layers show the *design* as drawn. Constructed
  works may differ in routing, depth, size, and detail. Do not treat this
  map as a record of what is in the ground.
- **Not for excavation.** Never rely on this map to locate underground
  services before digging. Conduct on-site service location.
- **Positional accuracy is not warranted.** Data was converted from CAD
  (UTM Zone 58S) to WGS84. Contours are simplified (~0.4 m tolerance) for
  web display.
- **Currency.** Source drawings date from the NSUDP design period; contour
  data is labelled 2023. No commitment is made to keep this map current.
- **Attributes are partial.** Pipe diameters are shown only where recorded
  in the source drawings.
